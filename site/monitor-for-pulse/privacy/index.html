Privacy Policy

Monitor for Pulse for iPhone, Android and Mac

The short version: we don't collect your data. The app has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking, and it has no server of ours to send anything to.

What the app handles

  • Your Pulse address and API token. You enter these. They are stored only on your device: the token in the iOS or macOS Keychain, or Android's secure storage.
  • Monitoring data from your Pulse server, such as names and status of machines, resource use, temperatures, backup times and alerts. The app fetches this directly from the server you choose, over HTTPS. A recent copy is kept on your device so it can show something straight away. On a Mac, the app shares that copy with its widget.

None of this is sent to us or to any third party. Your Pulse server's own privacy practices are up to whoever runs it.

Deleting your data

Choose Disconnect in the app's settings to remove the saved address, token and cached data, or uninstall the app.

App stores and support

Apple and Google operate their stores and may give developers aggregated, anonymous statistics and crash reports under their own privacy policies. If you email us for support, we use your email address and message only to answer you and delete them when they're no longer needed.

Children

The app is a tool for people running their own servers and isn't directed at children. It collects no information from anyone.

Your rights and contact

The app is published by HB En Lang och En Kort, Sweden. Since the app collects no personal data, we normally hold none about you. If you've contacted us, you can ask for access to or deletion of that correspondence, and you can complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY).
